Why O5

Five elements. One practice.

O5 is named for the five elements: air, space, fire, water and earth. Each class draws on one of them, so your body learns to move with strength, flow and ease, and your mind gets a place to slow down.

Who it's for

Every body is welcome

You do not need to be flexible, strong or experienced. Our classes are guided step by step, with options to make each movement easier or more challenging.

  • First-timers who have never set foot in a studio
  • Desk workers with a stiff neck, shoulders or lower back
  • Runners, gym-goers and players looking to move better
  • Anyone who wants a calm hour that is just for them

If you have an injury or a medical condition, please tell us when you join the list so we can guide you well.

Good to know

Common questions

When does the studio open?

We open on 1 November 2026 in Patiala. Waiting-list members hear first and get early access to booking.

Do I need any experience?

Not at all. Classes are suitable for complete beginners, and your instructor will offer variations as you progress.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable, stretchy clothing that lets you move freely. For Pilates, grip socks are helpful. Bring a water bottle and arrive about ten minutes early.

Is aerial yoga safe for beginners?

Yes. The silk hammock supports your weight, and your instructor teaches each pose step by step. Please mention any injuries beforehand.

How do I book a class?

Join the waiting list on this page. We will share timings and booking details with you before opening day. You can also reach us on Instagram or the contact page.

What are the three class types?

Pilates for core-led strength, aerial yoga for supported stretching and spine relief, and wall yoga for rope-assisted alignment. See the classes page for more.
