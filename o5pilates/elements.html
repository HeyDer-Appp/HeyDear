The philosophy

Pancha Mahabhuta

The number in our name is the five great elements. They shape our space, our classes and the way we teach.

Air · Vayu

Breath, lightness and freedom of movement. In our aerial yoga practice you lift off the ground, lengthen the spine and let every breath create space.

Practised in: Aerial Yoga

Space · Akasha

The stillness between movements. Space is where focus settles, posture is refined and the mind finally quiets.

Practised in: Pilates

Fire · Agni

The warm, steady heat of the core. Fire is strength, discipline and the energy that builds with each controlled repetition.

Practised in: Pilates

Water · Jala

Flow, fluidity and release. Water guides transitions from one pose to the next, softening tension and restoring ease.

Practised in: Pilates & Aerial Yoga

Earth · Prithvi

Grounding, stability and roots. Earth is the wall at your back and the floor beneath you, holding you steady while you stretch.

Practised in: Wall Yoga
