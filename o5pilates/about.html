About O5

A quieter way to move

O5 Pilates & Yoga Studio is a calm, light-filled space in Patiala for people who want strength without noise.

Our story

Space to breathe, room to grow

We started O5 with a simple idea: a studio should feel like an exhale. Soft light, warm sand tones, natural textures and nothing rushed.

Pilates, aerial yoga and wall yoga sit side by side, each linked to one of the five elements, so you can pick the practice that matches how you feel today.
